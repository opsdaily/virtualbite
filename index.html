What this model does and doesn't count
  • Uses the plan tier you pick for each app. Published 2026 rates: DoorDash 15/25/30% delivery and 6% pickup, Uber Eats 20/25/30% and 7% pickup, Grubhub 5/15/20% marketing plus 10% if they drive.
  • All three charge card processing on top of commission, not inside it. We count roughly 2.9% + 30¢, so the effective take rate you see is higher than the headline percentage.
  • On a direct order you still pay processing — 2.9% + 10¢ on our platform — so we subtract that too. Processing largely cancels out; the commission is the real saving.
  • Subtracts the Growth plan at $549/mo and the $2,900 one-time build before showing any gain.
  • Assumes a six-month ramp. Nobody converts a third of their app orders in week one.
  • Does not count driver cost if you don't already deliver, or the new revenue from loyalty, email and SMS to guests you now own. Those push in opposite directions, so we leave both out.
